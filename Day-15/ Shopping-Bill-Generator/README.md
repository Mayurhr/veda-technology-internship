# Shopping Bill Generator

## Description

This is a simple Python program that accepts product names, quantities, and prices and generates a formatted shopping bill.

The bill includes the subtotal, discount, tax, and final amount.

## Objective

The objective of this task is to practice:

- Lists
- Dictionaries
- Loops
- Functions
- Conditional statements
- Calculations
- Formatted output

## Features

- Accepts multiple products
- Stores product details
- Calculates individual product totals
- Calculates subtotal
- Applies discount
- Calculates tax
- Displays the final shopping bill

## Discount Rules

- Subtotal of ₹5000 or more → 10% discount
- Subtotal of ₹2000 or more → 5% discount
- Below ₹2000 → No discount

## Tax

A 5% tax is calculated after applying the discount.
